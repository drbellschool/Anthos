SKILL-CODE · i-nontransactional
Quality standard

8/10 release gate

A scene must pass continuity, advancement, coaching impact, character autonomy, nonrepetition, research skills, realism, humor, safety and evidence integrity.

Blocking failures: invented irreversible facts, uncited score changes, repeated stale announcements, privacy leaks, or coercion presented as success.

Important: 8/10 is a test threshold, not a promise that every real-life interaction will be perfect.
