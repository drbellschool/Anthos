THREEFOLD MESSAGING PROGRAM

A little wonder,
delivered thoughtfully.

Threefold is the opt-in SMS experience within Anthos, operated by Adam Bell, sole proprietor. Once launched, each adult spouse can choose to participate using their own mobile number and manage their own consent.

Messages are intended to include relationship prompts, date-night ideas, shared challenges, requested reminders, program updates, and conversational replies.
