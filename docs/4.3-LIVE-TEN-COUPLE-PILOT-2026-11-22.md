### Quality concerns discovered by reading the *real* text

The automated world-state pass **is not** the required independent 8/10 human review. For example:
- The otherwise responsive “national microwave repair holiday” line ends with “before we need a new one,” even though the fixed episode premise states that Jill **already purchased** the replacement. This is a subtle coherence blemish despite no committed event violation.
- In the high-conflict-to-repair case, one Jill message refers to her private friend in the third person as “the coach.” That is an unnatural fourth-wall break. Dialogue needs a separate naturalness/character-voice audit even when all mechanical assertions pass.
- The story ledger still summarizes some Jack/Jill interpersonal exchanges rather than preserving a complete direct spouse-to-spouse transcript; qualitative evaluation should not overclaim what those events prove.

**Therefore:** engineering live-model smoke **PASSED**; mandatory human-reviewed **8/10 quality certification remains unverified**. Do not merge PR #135 or lift the release gate on the basis of this run alone.
