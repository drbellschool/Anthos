Terms & SMS Terms

Effective October 20, 2026

About the service: Anthos is developing Threefold, an opt-in marriage adventure and messaging program for adults. The current public experience is a local-only interactive demonstration. SMS enrollment is not open while the carrier campaign remains under review.

Eligibility and voluntary participation

Use of the program is limited to adults age 18 or older. Participation must be voluntary for each person. When SMS enrollment becomes available, you must be the subscriber or customary user of the number you register and authorized to consent for that number. One partner cannot opt the other partner in.

Threefold SMS program

Once enrollment opens, you may opt in by texting JOIN to +1 (903) 609-0465 after reviewing these disclosures. By doing so, you consent to receive recurring automated and conversational text messages from Threefold by Anthos. Message content may include relationship prompts, Love Whispers, date-night suggestions, interactive challenges, reminders you request, program updates, and replies to messages you initiate.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. You may also contact drbell@lonestarteacher.com for assistance.

Current demo and feature availability

The preview at anthos.love/experience/ is not a live subscription or a linked couple account. Demo progress is stored on your device, and sharing actions do not send messages. Sophia, Paraclete, reservations, live AI conversations, individualized SMS, partner synchronization, payment processing, and production enrollment are planned features and may not yet be available.

Educational and recreational purpose

Threefold offers educational and recreational relationship prompts, activities, and fictional scenarios. It is not psychotherapy, professional counseling, medical services, legal advice, or crisis support. It should not be used to address abusive, coercive, or dangerous situations or emergencies. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Choices, spending, and safety

You remain responsible for your decisions, transportation, purchases, plans, and communications. Suggested adventures are invitations, not requirements. No reservations or purchases are made automatically by the current demo. Future booking or payment features will require separate, clearly authorized actions.

Partner privacy

Some experiences may be shared and others private. When sharing features become available, choose intentionally what you share and never submit content you do not have the right to disclose. Each participant must control their own opt-in and opt-out preferences.

Acceptable use

Do not use Anthos or Threefold to threaten, harass, impersonate, exploit, violate privacy or other rights, or engage in unlawful activity. Access may be restricted as reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.

AI and changing features

Future AI-generated suggestions can be inaccurate or incomplete. Features, frequency, and availability may change as Anthos develops. No guarantee is made that the experience will produce any particular relationship result.
